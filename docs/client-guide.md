# Running your site

Everything on this page you can do yourself, without needing to ask. To get in,
scroll to the bottom of any page on the site and click **Admin**, then enter your
passcode.

---

## Classes and events

Add an event from **Admin → Events**. Fill in the title, date, time, location,
price, and how many seats there are. Leave the price at zero and the event is
free — guests are confirmed the moment they sign up. Give it a price and they
check out through Square instead.

Untick **Published** while you are still working on something. It stays hidden
until you tick it.

### Cover images

The cover is the picture that appears at the top of the event on the calendar
page. Whatever you upload is now shown **in full** — nothing is trimmed off the
edges — so a flyer with the date and details written into it stays readable.

A couple of things worth knowing when you make one:

- **Landscape works best.** A picture wider than it is tall fills the card
  neatly from edge to edge. If you are designing something specifically for the
  website, around **1200 x 900** or **1200 x 800** is ideal.
- **Tall flyers are fine too** — they just sit in the middle of the card with a
  soft cream band down either side. Nothing is cut off, it simply doesn't fill
  the full width. If you'd rather it filled the card, make a wider version.
- **Keep the important words away from the very edges**, and aim for at least
  1000 pixels across so it stays sharp on a big screen.
- The picture on the card is a taster, not the only place the details live. The
  date, time, location, and price all appear underneath it as text, so nobody
  has to squint at the flyer to work out when something is.

You can see exactly how a cover will look before you publish — the small preview
beside **Cover image** in the admin panel shows the whole picture, the same way
the card will.

## Private lessons and private events

These two pages work differently to the calendar. Nobody books and pays up
front. They read the page, fill in a short form telling you what they're after,
and you take it from there.

The lessons form asks how experienced they are, when suits them, and where
they'd like to meet. The events form asks what the occasion is, where they're
thinking of holding it, and which dates they have in mind. Both ask for name,
email, phone, and how many people.

When someone submits one, they get an email straight away confirming you've
received it and making clear nothing has been charged. You get an email with all
their details, which you can reply to directly — it goes to them, not to us.

### Agreeing a price and getting paid

Open **Private Lessons** or **Private Events** in the admin panel. Every enquiry
is listed, newest first. Click **Set price & send link** on one and you can set:

- **Price** — what you've agreed to charge
- **Status** — where things stand
- **Agreed date, time, and location**
- **Private notes** — only you ever see these

Click **Save** to record it.

When you're ready to be paid, use **Send a payment link** lower down. Add a short
note if you like, then click the button. That emails them a secure Square link
for the price you set. You never handle card details, and nothing is charged
until they choose to pay. Once they pay you'll get an email, and the enquiry
moves to **Paid** on its own.

### Setting the date

Filling in the agreed date and saving is what emails the customer.

If they haven't paid yet, that email **holds the date** for them and includes the
payment link, so they get the details and the way to pay in one place. If they've
already paid, it simply confirms everything. Changing the date later sends an
updated email; saving twice without changing anything won't send a duplicate.

## Changing the wording on your pages

The wording on your About page, and on both the private lessons and private
events pages, is yours to edit without needing anyone.

In the admin panel click **Page Text**. Everything is grouped by page and each
box is labelled with where it appears. Change what you like and click **Save
changes** — the site updates immediately.

Two boxes behave slightly differently:

- **"Perfect for"** — put one item on each line.
- **The four highlighted blocks** — leave a blank line between each one. The
  first line of a block is its heading, everything after it is the description.
  You can add or remove blocks freely.

If an edit ever goes wrong, **Reset to original wording** puts it all back.

## Where your emails go

Anything a customer sends lands in your inbox, and replies go straight back to
them. Emails the site sends on your behalf come from an address on your own
domain so they don't get caught by spam filters — you don't need to do anything
with that address, and you can't receive mail at it. Always reply from your own
email.
