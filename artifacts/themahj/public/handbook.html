Getting in

Everything on this page you can do yourself, without needing to ask.

Scroll to the bottom of any page on your site and click Admin, then enter your passcode. That is the same panel wherever you are on the site.

The calendar

Classes and events

Add one from Admin → Events. Fill in the title, date, time, location, price, and how many seats there are.

Leave the price at zero and the event is free — guests are confirmed the moment they sign up. Give it a price and they check out through Square instead.

Untick Published while you are still working on something. It stays hidden from the calendar until you tick it.

The calendar

Cover images

The cover is the picture at the top of an event on the calendar page. Whatever you upload is shown in full — nothing is trimmed off the edges — so a flyer with the date and details written into it stays readable.

A few things worth knowing when you make one:

  • Landscape works best. A picture wider than it is tall fills the card neatly from edge to edge. If you are designing something specifically for the website, around 1200 × 900 or 1200 × 800 is ideal.
  • Tall flyers are fine too. They sit in the middle of the card with a soft cream band down either side. Nothing is cut off — it simply doesn't fill the full width. If you'd rather it did, make a wider version.
  • Keep the important words away from the very edges, and aim for at least 1000 pixels across so it stays sharp on a large screen.
  • The picture is a taster, not the record. The date, time, location and price all appear underneath it as text, so nobody has to squint at the flyer to work out when something is.
Check before you publish

The small preview beside Cover image in the admin panel shows the whole picture, exactly the way the card will. If it looks right there, it will look right on the site.

Enquiries

Private lessons and events

These two pages work differently to the calendar. Nobody books and pays up front. Someone reads the page, fills in a short form telling you what they're after, and you take it from there.

The lessons form asks how experienced they are, when suits them, and where they'd like to meet. The events form asks what the occasion is, where they're thinking of holding it, and which dates they have in mind. Both ask for name, email, phone, and how many people.

When someone submits one, they get an email straight away confirming you've received it and making clear that nothing has been charged. You get an email with all their details — and you can reply to it directly, because it goes to them, not to us.

Enquiries

Agreeing a price and getting paid

Open Private Lessons or Private Events in the admin panel. Every enquiry is listed, newest first.

  1. Click Set price & send link on the enquiry you're working on.
  2. Fill in what you've agreed, then click Save. Nothing is sent to the customer at this stage except the date — see below.
  3. When you're ready to be paid, use Send a payment link lower down. Add a short note if you like, then click the button.

The fields you can set are:

Price
What you have agreed to charge.
Status
Where things stand. Usually this updates itself.
Agreed date, time and location
Filling in the date is what emails the customer.
Private notes
Only you ever see these.

Sending the link emails them a secure Square link for the price you set. You never handle card details, and nothing is charged until they choose to pay. Once they pay you'll get an email, and the enquiry moves to Paid on its own.

Setting the date

Filling in the agreed date and saving is what emails the customer.

If they haven't paid yet, that email holds the date for them and includes the payment link, so they get the details and the way to pay in one place. If they've already paid, it simply confirms everything.

Changing the date later sends them an updated email. Saving twice without changing anything won't send a duplicate.

Enquiries

What the labels mean

An enquiry moves down this list as things progress. You rarely need to set these yourself.

  • New requestThey've been in touch. Nothing agreed, nothing charged.
  • Payment link sentYou've quoted a price and emailed them a link.
  • PaidThe money has arrived in your Square account.
  • ScheduledPaid, with a date in the diary.
  • CompletedIt has happened. Set this yourself when you're done.
  • CancelledCalled off. Refunds are handled in Square, not here.

Your pages

Changing your wording

The wording on your About page, and on both the private lessons and private events pages, is yours to edit without needing anyone.

In the admin panel click Page Text. Everything is grouped by page, and each box is labelled with where it appears. Change what you like and click Save changes — the site updates immediately.

Two boxes behave a little differently:

  • “Perfect for” — put one item on each line.
  • The four highlighted blocks — leave a blank line between each one. The first line of a block is its heading, everything after it is the description. You can add or remove blocks freely.
Nothing here can break the site

The page supplies all its own styling, so an edit can't disturb the layout. And if something ever goes wrong, Reset to original wording puts it all back.

Behind the scenes

Where your email goes

Anything a customer sends lands in your inbox, and your replies go straight back to them.

Emails the site sends on your behalf come from an address on your own domain, so they don't get caught by spam filters. You don't need to do anything with that address, and you can't receive mail at it — always reply from your own email.
